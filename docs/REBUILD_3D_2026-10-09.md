# 3D robot pivot — Unitree A1 + Humanoid (2026-10-09)

The report/poster previously centred on HalfCheetah (2D). That cannot demonstrate
lateral-kick recovery or 3D multi-embodiment transfer. All headline results are
moved to 3D robots: the 12-DoF Unitree A1 quadruped and the 17-DoF Humanoid.
HalfCheetah is kept only as a secondary benchmark.

## Unitree A1 (12-DoF quadruped)

Dataset: `data/unitree_a1_trajectories.npz` (200 trajectories, 500 steps, obs 35D,
action 12D), collected with a CPG trot controller plus noise and periodic kicks.
Training: `configs/unitree_a1_aug.yaml`, 40 epochs, batch 512, seed 42.
Log: `results/train_unitree.log`. Val loss 4.00 -> 0.449; action loss 0.108.
Checkpoint: `checkpoints/rebuild_unitree/unitree_a1/best_model.pt`.

### Real lateral-kick recovery (measured, not drawn)

`python scripts/evaluate_unitree_kick.py --checkpoint .../unitree_a1/best_model.pt`
Output: `results/unitree_kick.json`, `results/unitree_kick_high.json`.
A genuine lateral force impulse (`qfrc_applied` on the trunk) is applied for 3
control steps at t=200; peak torso roll, full-episode survival and recovery time
are measured over 3 episodes.

| Lateral force | Peak torso roll | Survival | Recovery |
| --: | --: | --: | --: |
| 50 | 7.0 deg | 100% | < 0.1 s |
| 100 | 8.0 deg | 100% | < 0.1 s |
| 200 | 25.0 deg | 100% | < 0.1 s |
| 400 | 51.1 deg | **0%** (falls at step 209) | — |

The earlier hand-drawn "30 N / 33 deg / recovers at 1.0 s" figure is removed:
it was synthetic. The quadruped withstands a measured lateral kick up to 200 in
model force units and falls at 400.

## Humanoid (17-DoF, 348D state)

Dataset: `data/humanoid_standup_foundation.npz` (240 trajectories, 1000 steps,
obs 348D, action 17D). `TrajectoryCollector.load_dataset` now computes
return-to-go from rewards when the archive omits it.
Training: `configs/humanoid_v5_aug.yaml`, 30 epochs, batch 256.
Log: `results/train_humanoid.log`. Checkpoint: `checkpoints/rebuild_unitree/humanoid/`.

## Honest notes

- The Unitree A1 and Humanoid datasets are **CPG/scripted collectors with noise**,
  not expert RL demonstrations. Action loss is a prediction metric; closed-loop
  return quality depends on the data.
- "50 N" is not an independently calibrated newton value; magnitudes are reported
  in the same model force units the collector used, so they are comparable across
  runs but should not be quoted as a physical newton measurement without a load-cell
  calibration.
